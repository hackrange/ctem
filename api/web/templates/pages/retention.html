Data retention

When a host counts as gone, and how long remediated findings are kept

{% include "partials/admin_tabs.html" %}
Gone hosts

"Seen" is any sign of life: a scan, an EDR check-in, a directory logon, a finding a tool still reports, a cloud inventory row, a probe answer. Nothing is removed on a guess: every unseen host is put on the network probe's list first, and only one that stays silent to it for that many days without a single answer (no ping, nothing on the common ports) loses its findings; any answer restarts the count. With 30 and 7 that's 37 days at least. A host with no internal address can't be checked, so it keeps them. The asset record stays.

Remediated findings

After that it is counted into a daily summary (found and fixed per day, by source, kind of work, severity and KEV, with its days to fix) and then removed, every day at 04:15 UTC. The vulnerability trend, fix counts and mean time to remediate keep their whole history from the summary, and each device keeps a year of its trend from its daily counts. A view narrowed to a device type, a tag scope or a repository covers only the days kept here. Risk score history older than a year keeps one entry per week.
